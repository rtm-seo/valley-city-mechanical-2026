Valley City Mechanical

Period Oct 1, 2025 to Oct 15, 2026 Prepared Oct 16, 2026

01Organic performance summary

Organic leads are down from the summer peak, but they are well ahead of last year. The drop follows the same seasonal curve as 2025. The risk to watch is heating season: the site barely ranks for the heating terms that will drive the next five months.

Bottom line

Year over year, organic search is up on every measure. Over the last 12 full months, organic sessions grew 18% and organic leads rose from 86 to 201. Leads fell from 30 in July to 24 in September and are pacing about 8 for October, but last year the same months went 15, 10 and 8. The slowdown is the shoulder season between AC and heating, not an SEO collapse. The bigger risk is what comes next: non-brand heating pages sit on page 2 to 5 of Google going into the busiest heating months.

What the month-over-month view shows

  • Last year, July to September 2025 produced 15, 5 and 10 organic leads, then 8 in October. The same summer-to-fall slide is happening this year from a much higher base.
  • May to July 2026 was the strongest stretch in the 12 months (27, 26 and 30 leads). Lead rate jumped from roughly 6 to 12% in the cooler months to 16 to 26% in summer as AC repair calls came in from mobile searchers.
  • August was the real dip: leads fell 37% (30 to 19) while sessions rose 10%, so traffic held and fewer visitors converted.
  • September recovered to 24 leads (+26%) on fewer sessions (104), the second-best lead rate of the year at 23% (May was 26%).
  • October is the soft month. Sessions are on pace for about 139, ahead of September, but only 4 leads have come in so far. October 2025 also produced just 8 organic leads.

04What is actually slipping

The lead numbers are seasonal, but Search Console shows one gap that will cost leads this winter and two trends worth watching.

Brand-name search impressions

Queries containing "valley", Google Search Console

Brand impressions ran 930 to 2,200 a month through August, then dropped to 406 in September. Brand clicks stayed between 14 and 31 a month.

Total Google search clicks

All queries, Google Search Console

Clicks peaked at 84 in January and have slid to 35 in September, the low of the period. October is pacing about 55 (Oct 1 to 13).

High priority

Non-brand rankings are too weak for heating season

Outside the brand name, most organic clicks come from a few blog posts. The money pages sit too low to earn clicks:

  • Homepage: 47,082 impressions, average position 29, 0.7% CTR.
  • "furnace repair near me": 5,053 impressions at position 16.5 with 1 click. This is the single biggest heating opportunity in the data.
  • Grand Rapids service-area page: 38,282 impressions at position 53.
  • Hudsonville, Zeeland and Holland pages: 83,000 impressions combined at positions 13 to 28, with 14 clicks.
  • Water heater and tankless pages: 34,800 impressions combined at positions 28 to 34, 10 clicks.

The page with the second-most clicks is a Michigan weather article (17,754 impressions, 55 clicks). It ranks well but brings in visitors who are not looking for HVAC service.

Watch

Brand search impressions dropped in September, but brand clicks held

"valley city mechanical" is the site's top query: 224 of the roughly 740 Google clicks in this period. Impressions for brand searches fell from 1,052 in August to 406 in September, yet brand clicks barely moved: 74 from June to September 2026 against 77 in the same months of 2025, and October is pacing about 21 against 24 last October. People who search the name are still finding the site. Keep an eye on GBP Insights for branded searches and calls, since calls placed straight from the Business Profile do not show up in GA4.

Watch

Overall impressions are trending down from spring

Google impressions averaged about 50,000 a month from October through February, spiked to 90,426 in March, and have trended down to 34,767 in September. Average position has actually improved (from about 29 to 23), which suggests Google is showing the site for fewer loosely related queries, not that rankings dropped across the board. Impressions cannot be compared year over year: Google changed how it counts them in mid-September 2025, and daily impressions for the site fell from about 3,000 to under 1,000 within days.

05A measurement gap to confirm

Some of the "missing" leads may not be missing at all. This gap makes the October numbers harder to read and is worth confirming.

High priority

Phone taps fell across every channel in October, not just organic

Sitewide phone taps went from 190 in July to 147 in August, 117 in September and 6 in October 1 to 15. Paid search alone dropped from 89 phone taps in September to 5, even with 224 paid sessions. October 2025 was also slow (14 phone taps all month), so part of this is seasonal, but the paid drop is steep enough to test the tracking. The site now shows two numbers, (616) 591-4786 and (616) 253-9218. Confirm in Google Tag Manager that the phone tag fires for both.

06Action plan

Ordered by impact on lead flow over the next 90 days, starting with heating season.

  1. Rework the furnace repair page to target "furnace repair" in Byron Center and Grand Rapids, with the phone number and a short form above the fold.

  4. Re-run this report in January against January 2026 (14 organic leads, 146 sessions) to measure heating-season progress.
